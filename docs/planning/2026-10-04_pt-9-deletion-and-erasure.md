## 0. The finding this plan exists for

**Every store can create a campaign's data, and only one code path can remove any of it: a failed create undoing its own reservation.** `releaseCampaign` (`pg-brief-store.ts:496`) deletes a campaign row only while it has zero versions, and `deleteAssets` (`object-asset-store.ts:470`) only empties `inputs/`. No route ends in `.delete.ts` except `draft.delete.ts` and the provider-key revoke. Three facts make "just add a DELETE route" wrong:

1. **Half the per-campaign tables are keyed by the SLUG, not the campaign id** [code]: `decision`, `decision_set`, `report`, `pool` and `job` have `campaign_id text` = the slug (D178; `0002`, `0003`, `0005`, `0006`). Deleting the `campaign` row frees the slug (`unique (org_id, slug)`, `0004`). The next campaign that derives the same slug then **inherits the old campaign's report, decisions and pool**. That is a cross-campaign leak, and in a team-scoped org it can cross team boundaries.
2. **Renders are not fenced** [code]. Only `progressJob`/`completeJob`/`failJob`, decisions and reports check the lease (`pg-job-store.ts:334-368`, `decisions.ts:90-112`). `ObjectExporter` puts and `copyAssets` copies are plain writes, and a run resolves `renderTarget` once (`pipeline.ts:465`). So a purge that runs beside a live run, or beside a save that is copying assets, can be followed by objects that nothing will ever delete.
3. **Better Auth's `/organization/delete` is live today** [code]. `options.ts:147-199` never sets `disableOrganizationDeletion`. Better Auth 1.7.6 (`plugins/organization/routes/crud-org.mjs:239-286`) lets an org owner call it. On an org with data it fails on our non-cascading FKs (a 500). On an org without data it succeeds and cascades `member`, `team` and `invitation`. Either way the org is deleted outside any purge.

**Checked and not a defect.**
- **Hidden campaigns already answer 404 on every campaign route** [code]: every route under `routes/campaigns/` that takes a campaign resolves it through `resolveCampaignRef`, `campaignMeta`, `findBriefById` or `campaignVisibility` (counted per file). One reader filter (H1) therefore turns a tombstoned campaign into a 404 everywhere. `jobs/[id].get.ts` reads by job id inside the org, and answers 404 once the purge removes the job row.
- **`deletePrefix` is idempotent and all-or-nothing on a bad listing** (`S3ObjectStore.ts:210-234`). A purge step can be re-run after a crash.
- **`usage` carries no user and no campaign** (`0007`, `0009`). Billing retention therefore does not conflict with erasure.

| **D230** | **Three operations, three meanings.** (a) **Campaign deletion** hard-deletes the campaign's content: every row, every object under its prefix, its drafts and its pointers. | **D231** | **A tombstone plus a durable deletion queue, swept. No restore in PT-9.** `campaign.deleted_at` (and `deleted_by`) hides the campaign at once. ## 3. The target shape

```
DELETE /campaigns/:id ─▶ resolveCampaignRef (404 hidden) ─▶ authorise (403) ─▶ tombstone tx
      campaign row FOR UPDATE · active job? → 409 · deleted_at=now · insert deletion(not_before)
                                                                     │  202 {deletionId}
sweeper (bin/purge.ts, CronJob) ── claim deletion: lease (claimed_until=now()+lease), committed ─┘
   campaign: job active? retry later ─▶ free bytes (prefix | fs trees) ─▶ tx{ slug-keyed rows,
             brief_version, campaign (cascades asset/draft/last_opened) } ─▶ re-list prefix ─▶ purged_at
   org:      tombstone org ─▶ enqueue each campaign ─▶ org rows ─▶ deletePrefix(org/<org>/) ─▶ anonymise org row
   reconcile: prefixes with no campaign row · input objects with no asset row (> 1 h old)
erase:user (CLI) ─▶ sole-owner guard ─▶ one tx: actors→token, drafts/pointers/verification/invitee rows,
                    "user" (cascade) ─▶ memberCount ─▶ deletion(kind=user, subject=token)
```

**State per campaign:** `live` → (`DELETE`) `tombstoned` (invisible; slug reserved) → (sweep) `purged` (no row, no object; slug free). There is no edge back to `live` in PT-9.

**Retained after each operation:**

| Operation | Deleted | Anonymised | Retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaign | all rows keyed by its uuid or slug; `org/<org>/campaign/<uuid>/`; fs trees | — | the `deletion` row (uuid, timestamps, requester); `usage` (never campaign-keyed); cache entries until TTL (D242) |
| Org | every campaign (as above); `provider_key`, `member`, `team`, `team_member`, `invitation`, `draft`, `last_opened`; `org/<org>/` | the `org` row (name, slug, logo, metadata) | `usage` rows and the org tombstone, both for 13 months (Q5, resolved 2026-10-04), then deleted together; `deletion` rows |
| User | `"user"`, `session`, `account`, `member`, `team_member`, invitations sent or received, `verification`, `draft`, `last_opened` | `brief_version.actor`, `decision.actor`, `provider_key.created_by`, `campaign.deleted_by`, `deletion.requested_by` → `erased:<uuid>` | the content they authored (the org's); `usage` (no user column) |

## 5. What this plan refuses

- **No restore/trash UI** in PT-9 (D231). A tombstone is a crash-safe state, not an undo.
- **No fencing of every render put.** Exclusion (D235) plus a reconciler (D239) is cheaper than a fence on every object write, and as complete.
- **No transaction held across an object-store call** (15-connection budget, D174a).
- **No deletion through Better Auth's endpoints** (D241). Our purge owns order and retention.
- **No `audit_event` table in PT-9** (L2). PT-2 owns it, and the `deletion` row is the deletion record until then.
- **No erasure of content the org owns** because its author was erased (D230 c).

## 6. Definition of done

1. A deleted campaign leaves **no row** keyed by its uuid or slug in any of the 10 per-campaign tables, and **no object under `campaignPrefix`**, after one sweep. This is asserted end to end through the routes (9g), on the in-memory store always and on SeaweedFS when `TEST_S3_ENDPOINT` is set. *(Amends parent DoD 8 per D242; Q6 resolved 2026-10-04 to the default.)*
2. Re-creating a deleted campaign's name never surfaces its report, decisions or pool (C1, 9g).
3. A purge interrupted at any step converges when re-run (9g).
4. DELETE racing generate admits exactly one (9c, 9f, real PG).
5. Every per-column erasure test passes, and a whole-database text scan finds neither the erased email nor the user id (9l).
6. `/api/auth/organization/delete` answers 404 (9a0).
7. The carried residuals each have a red-on-mutation interleaving test (9d, 9e, 9i, 9j, 9k).
8. Before a production claim, **B2's delete semantics are verified and the lifecycle rule is set** (M4). That is an owner check, not a lane.
